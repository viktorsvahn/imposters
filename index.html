How it works

  • Hold Ctrl+Shift and point at a tile edge: a + slides along it. Click to split the tile there — a + on the right/left edge splits it into upper and lower tiles, on the bottom/top edge into left and right. Snaps to ½ ⅓ ¼ and to other tiles' edges. Ctrl+Alt+Shift splits into equal parts instead — as many as Parts says (keys 2–9). Ctrl+Shift-click empty space to fill it.
  • Levels: divide the poster into sections first, then Enter a section (double-click, or E) and divide it further. Everything outside is dimmed; Esc or a click outside goes back up. Each divided tile has its own inner margin and gutter, and its tiles reflow when it is resized.
  • Bleed (Tile panel): the background runs to the paper edge wherever the tile touches the margin, while its text and inner tiles stay within the margin. The header and footer use this.
  • Header / Footer checkboxes (Paper panel, or the Section panel inside a tile) add or remove a full-width band at the top/bottom; the other tiles make room. Inside tiles they are off by default.
  • Text columns (in the Tile panel) flow one text into several columns inside a tile.
  • Drag a gap between tiles to resize both sides at once.
  • Drag on empty paper to draw a tile.
  • + Text / + Image fill the largest empty area.
  • Split a tile to subdivide it; Fill grows it to its neighbours.
  • Tiles snap to margins, columns, other tiles and the gutter between them.
  • Click text to type — anywhere, even inside divided tiles. Drag to move a tile; Esc stops typing. Go inside a tile to create tiles there: double-click it, or Enter ⤵, E, or Ctrl+Enter (also while typing). Everything outside is greyed out; Split then divides that tile. Double-click a handle to grow that side.
  • Drop or paste images onto the paper or a tile.
  • Shift-click to multi-select. Arrows nudge (Shift = 10 mm), Alt+arrows grow.
  • Design tab: colours for paper, tiles, headers and footers, and the text styles offered while writing (tick, rename, restyle).
